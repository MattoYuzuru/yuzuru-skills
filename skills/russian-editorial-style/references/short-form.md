# Short-form and social writing

Identify one primary message or reader action. Front-load the information needed
to decide whether to continue, respond, or act. Keep only context that changes
interpretation.

## Channel fit

- Respect the requested length, format, links, mentions, and accessibility needs.
- Use line breaks, bullets, or an opening label only when they improve scanning.
- Keep a call to action when the communication has a real action; do not invent one.
- Use supported concrete detail. Never manufacture urgency, authority, popularity,
  customer stories, metrics, or personal experience.
- Treat hooks, emoji, questions, and hashtags as optional channel conventions,
  not requirements. Their presence does not make a text engaging or human.

For a short text, every stylistic addition competes with meaning. Prefer one
clear revision over several variants unless the user asks for options. Preserve
the author's form of address and emotional intensity; do not inflate either.

Before returning, check that shortening did not remove the condition, limitation,
attribution, or practical detail that makes the message true.
