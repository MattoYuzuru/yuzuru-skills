# Discovery Decision Framework

Score only when scores clarify a decision; do not manufacture arithmetic precision.

Consider:

- importance and frequency of the user job;
- severity of the current workaround;
- fit of existing products and open source;
- audience access, literacy, privacy, and regional constraints;
- unique capability or distribution advantage;
- build and ongoing maintenance burden;
- security, legal, and operational exposure;
- reversibility and cost of the next experiment.

Use recommendation labels from the primary discovery skill's output contract. Explain the
dominant reasons in prose and list at most three decision-changing unknowns.
