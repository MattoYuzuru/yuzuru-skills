# Discovery Research

Answer a bounded discovery question with current evidence. Read
`references/research-funnel.md` before a substantial market, regional, or feasibility scan.

## Workflow

1. State the decision the research must inform.
2. Convert it into unknowns and a query plan, including geography and time sensitivity.
3. Search categories, direct competitors, open source, regional alternatives, adjacent solutions,
   user complaints, feasibility signals, and credible failure stories as relevant.
4. Prefer official and primary sources for current capabilities, access, law, pricing, licensing,
   maintenance, and technical claims.
5. Record facts and inferences separately in an evidence ledger.
6. Compare alternatives by the target job, audience constraints, total operating burden, and gaps.
7. Stop when marginal research is unlikely to change a decision.
8. Return evidence, contradictions, coverage gaps, and remaining unknowns—not a link dump.

## Specialist lanes

Use subagents only for independent lanes. Suitable lanes include commercial competitors,
open-source alternatives, regional access and regulation, accessibility/technical literacy, and
technical feasibility. Give each lane exact questions and a source-quality requirement. The parent
must validate and synthesize their results.

## Guardrails

- Simulated user perspective is a hypothesis, not user research.
- Do not infer popularity, revenue, or market size without defensible evidence.
- Distinguish unavailable evidence from evidence of absence.
- Do not bypass authentication, regional blocks, or access controls.
- Mark volatile facts with source and access date.
