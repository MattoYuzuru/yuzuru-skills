# Visual Direction

Read `references/direction-method.md` for ambiguous aesthetics, a new brand surface, or advanced
motion/graphics.

## Workflow

1. Ground the direction in users, jobs, content, platform, brand, accessibility, and technical
   constraints.
2. Translate aesthetic words into hierarchy, density, grid, typography, shape, surface, depth,
   interaction, motion, color, performance, and fallback.
3. Research relevant current patterns and competitors without copying them.
4. Create two or three meaningfully different directions.
5. Compare user fit, navigation, content expression, technical cost, accessibility, performance,
   risks, and maintenance.
6. Recommend one direction and identify prototype questions.
7. Obtain confirmation before a large implementation when the direction remains materially open.

## Guardrails

- Do not generate ten near-identical mood boards.
- “Liquid Glass,” “terminal,” “editorial,” “spatial,” or “cinematic” is not an implementation spec.
- Distinctive does not mean busy, inaccessible, or fragile.
- Avoid default generated-dashboard and generic SaaS motifs.
