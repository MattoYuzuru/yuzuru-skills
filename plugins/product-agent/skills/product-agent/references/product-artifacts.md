# Product Artifact Selection

## Small change

Keep one section in the current product document:

- objective and affected users;
- behavior and failures;
- acceptance criteria;
- out of scope;
- questions and regression risks.

## Product or major workflow

Create a compact PRD and split files only when independent review or ownership requires it. Useful
separations include journeys, acceptance criteria, metrics, and traceability.

Every artifact should name status, assumptions, unresolved questions, and source evidence when the
artifact will be handed to another plugin. Follow the shared schemas only when the task size
justifies metadata.

## Architecture handoff

Provide:

- functional requirements and priorities;
- observable acceptance;
- latency, availability, privacy, residency, retention, audit, device, offline, and accessibility
  constraints;
- expected users and workload evidence when known;
- open semantics and decisions;
- no unapproved technology selection.
